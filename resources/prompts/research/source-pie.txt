Rules:
- Four labelled paragraphs, two to four sentences each:
  **Problem**: the problem or question the article addresses, and why it matters.
  **Idea**: the main idea, approach or hypothesis the authors bring to it.
  **Evidence**: how they support it: design, population or data (n), methods, comparisons, the key figures.
  **What the results mean**: what they found and what it implies, as the authors state it.
- Only what the article says: no opinion, no outside knowledge, nothing invented. Figures exactly as the article gives them. Write "not stated" for what the article does not say.
- Where the text carries a page marker `*— p. N —*`, end the sentence with (p. N).
- Write in {language}; keep technical terms and quotations in the language of the article.
- Answer with the four paragraphs only.
