Make flashcards for learning the scientific article "{title}" ({citation}): one question on the front, its answer on the back.

The article:
<source>
{source}
</source>

Write 10 to 25 cards, in exactly this form, a blank line between cards:

Q: the question
A: the answer

Rules:
- One fact a card: a definition, a sign, a threshold, a classification step, a figure, a recommendation. The question must be answerable without seeing the article.
- Answers short: a few words or one sentence. Figures exactly as the article gives them.
- Only what the article says: no outside knowledge, nothing invented.
- Where the text carries a page marker `*— p. N —*`, end the answer with (p. N).
- Write in {language}; keep technical terms in the language of the article when there is no exact equivalent.
- Answer with the cards only.
