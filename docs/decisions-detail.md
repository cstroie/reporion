## D48

**Report writes are current-format only; reads accept every format** (2026-10-15, owner's rule). Every save of a report writes the newest format only (`Storage` → `Support\Exams::normalize()`): no older shape, no copy kept for an older reader, no mix. Between reading an old report and saving it the app converts it (`normalize()` on the way to disk): an unsigned report is current at its next save, a signed one at its next correction. A new format ships with its read-fallback, its conversion and a test reading every historic shape, in the same commit. First use: exams: `modality`, `device`, `study_date` (the day) are the report's; title, time, region, template, accession, study and protocol are each exam's; nothing derived to the top level.
